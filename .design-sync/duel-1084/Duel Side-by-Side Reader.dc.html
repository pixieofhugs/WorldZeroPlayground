#1084 · turn 2 · the blueprint · two views

One side, or both sides

Turn 1 put both bodies on one duellist's page, under that duellist's vote. That reads as a page that can't decide whose it is. Splitting it fixes the confusion and gives the other eight factions a shape to inherit.

  • The side view (2a, 2b) — Wren's praxis, almost exactly as it is today. One body, one vote, one score. The duel block in the aside carries the standing and two ways out: the rival's page, or both sides together.
  • The combined view (2c, 2d, 2e) — genuinely two-up. Each entry carries its own vote control at the foot of its own column and its own link out. Nothing belongs to one duellist more than the other.

Also here: 2f the redacted Albescent duellist. Turn 1's artboards are kept below, superseded.

What this needs that does not exist yet

One new string, and only one: the link from a side view to the combined view. Everything else on both views is shipped catalog copy. Drawn below as ⟨duelCrossLink.readBothSides⟩ — the name is a proposal, the words are yours.

A route. The combined view is not a block on a praxis page; it is its own page for the duel. It takes the ground of whichever side you came from, per the 2026-08-27 ruling, and each duellist's faction stays on their sigil.

Bodies on the wire. get_duel_detail returns no praxis body by construction. The combined view needs both, fetched under can_view_praxis or added to the payload under the same guard.

2d · live — tap a name

One open at a time. You land on Wren expanded and Otho as a header; tapping Otho collapses Wren and opens him, so the two entries are never half-read at once and the standing stays in view above both.

What the third repetition changed

2f · nothing here needs redacting

§0 forbids naming a faction verbally anywhere in a duel, so no duel surface prints factionName() — the string isFactionRedacted() masks is not on the page to begin with.

And the mark is not a livery: AlbescentSigil is the labyrinth — three nested rings, each a band that turns back on itself — painted through a mask from --faction-default-rainbow-conic, the same spectrum an unaffiliated player wears, reinstated by owner ruling on exactly that ground. Drawn here to that description, painted from the same token. The shipped stencil is /factionMarks/labyrinth.svg, an absolute public/ path that the kit does not vendor, so FactionSigil slug="albescent" renders an empty span in this document and only in this document — in the app it is the real mark at any size.

The 64px threshold is AlbescentAvatar's RING_TURNS_AT, not the sigil's. The sigil scales to any size; the avatar's turning ring is simply absent below 64, which its own test says is above every mount in the app.

Copy — one new key, everything else shipped

New: one key, proposed as duelCrossLink.readBothSides — “Read both sides”. Faction-neutral like the rest of the duel set, and shaped after readTheirPraxis so the two links in the same panel read as a pair. Alternatives if that is too flat: “Read them side by side”, or “Both entries”.

Reused verbatim: duelCrossLink.label · .live + standing.* · .final + finalStanding.* · .finalNoContest · .wonByDefault · .readTheirPraxis · duelBanner.versus · detail.vote.heading and detail.vote.prompt for both casters · detail.taskRef.* · detail.filed.

The combined view's own page title is duelCrossLink.label — “The duel”, the same key the side view's aside panel already heads itself with.

One ruling this needs before it can be built

DuelCard is an aside block — flex: 0 0 330px on desktop, above the proof on mobile. Two praxis bodies cannot read side by side in 330px, so this reader cannot be a bigger DuelCard.

What these artboards propose: on a settled or resolved duel side, the reader is a full-sheet-width region in the main flow, and it subsumes the host's own Proof and Write-up sections rather than sitting beside them. The host's title, body and media are drawn exactly once — inside its own column of the reader — which is what keeps §6's "no two-pane workspace that re-renders what the detail page already owns" satisfied. The aside's duel panel is dropped on those two statuses; one duel block per page, as today.

Everything above and below is untouched: breadcrumb, byline, h1, owner actions, task reference, the score / vote / voters / flag rail, and comments beneath both columns.

The 64px gate, corrected

The gate is not on the sigil. FactionSigil takes any size and the labyrinth is a mask at contain, so one drawing serves 22px as well as 84px — every mark on these artboards is 24–26px and renders whole.

64 is AlbescentAvatar's RING_TURNS_AT: the turning ring is absent below it, and its own test records that 64 sits above every mount in the app on purpose. These discs are 34px, so the ring does not turn — and nothing is missing, because an Albescent disc below the gate is DefaultAvatar's disc exactly.

Copy audit — zero new strings

Every word on these artboards is shipped catalog copy, faction-neutral in all nine skins:

  • duelCrossLink.label — the section head, “The duel”. The same key DefaultPraxisDetail already passes as DuelCard's heading.
  • duelBanner.versus — the spine mark, “vs.”
  • duelCrossLink.live + standing.leads / standing.tied — the settled sentence, whole and untrimmed.
  • duelCrossLink.final + finalStanding.won / .tied, with finalNoContest and wonByDefault for the other two readings.
  • duelCrossLink.readTheirPraxis — the rival column's link out.
  • detail.taskRef.*, detail.filed, detail.sections.comments — the host page's own furniture, unchanged.

The one gap I expected — a votes-are-still-open footnote — is already inside duelCrossLink.live, which is why that sentence is reused whole rather than split into a leader line and a caveat.

Two detail.sections.* keys go unused on a duel side: Proof and Write-up. The reader's columns carry that content under The duel instead, so those two heads do not draw. If you would rather keep them, the columns can each carry their own pair — but that is four section heads in one region, and it reads as four sections rather than two entries.

Decisions I made, since the form came back unanswered

  • Ground: na / Unaffiliated — the host's dress, and the one DuelCard's default ink already paints. Any of the nine hosts substitutes by repointing the same tokens; nothing in the layout is na-specific except the spectrum, which is na's own tell.
  • Bodies: full, both sides, one page scroll. No independent column scrolling — the app has no two-pane detail page and the phone stacks.
  • Standing: two figures with the catalog sentence beneath. No bar, no delta badge, no leader crown.
  • Proof: in-column, in MediaGallery's own spectrum-edged frame — grid on desktop, column on phone, exactly as the archetype mounts it.
  • Winner mark on resolved: one spectrum rule and the sentence.

Two things in the brief the code contradicts

The 64px gate is the avatar's, not the sigil's. See 1e — the sigil renders at any size; RING_TURNS_AT = 64 belongs to AlbescentAvatar, and its test says 64 is deliberately above every mount that exists.

A stale comment survives §0's cleanup. frontend/src/utils/duelScenario.ts still names wow as “the one faction that overrides the duel-seal copy (praxis.json duelSeal.wow)” and steers duel fixtures around it. Those keys were deleted in #1909; the constant now excludes a faction for a reason that no longer exists. Worth a separate issue — I have not touched it.
