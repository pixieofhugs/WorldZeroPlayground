The metatask seal, pulled off the card

A metatask seal is the sticker a foreign faction’s bonus leaves on a task — it carries the issuing faction’s voice, type and palette, not the host card’s. Below: one seal shown in its natural habitat — a complete praxis card, desktop and mobile — then every issuing faction’s seal lifted out on neutral mounts for side-by-side study.
