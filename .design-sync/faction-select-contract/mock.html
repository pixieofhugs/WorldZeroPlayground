Faction Tile Contract
World Zero · faction select cards

One tile, eight dresses

The task cards all read band, hero, body, button. The faction tiles each grew their own list of text fields and their own place to put them. This page lists what differs today and proposes one set of slots every faction fills, with the paint, type and ornament left to the faction.

Mock-up · second pass

The contract in each faction's real dress

Each tile is rebuilt from the live card: its own sigil, ornament, ground, palette and typefaces, sampled in both themes. Only the arrangement is new. Every tile has the same seven slots in the same order.

Copy is the live catalog's. Dotted text marks the two lines still to be written, set as "Placeholder": the Everymen pitch and UA's locked line. Headcounts are example numbers. Albescent's locked tile is redacted as it is live, with every slot still in place; the mock ties that to the Locked state, where the real card ties it to whether the viewer is in on the secret.

The contract

Seven slots, same order, every faction

No new copy fields. Six text fields, which are exactly what the default card already has, plus one drawn ornament. A faction never adds a slot and never skips one.

  1. 1BandThe faction's mark, its name, and a drawn member seal. It comes in two forms and a faction picks one: a row (mark beside name: Coven, Snide, Ephemerists, Singularity, Everymen, Albescent) or a crest (mark above name: UA, WOW). Source: factions:names.
  2. 2MottoToday's tagline, promoted to the tile's display line. This is where each faction's typeface does its loudest work. Source: tagline.
  3. 3PitchToday's blurb. One to three sentences. It takes the spare height, so buttons line up across a row. Source: blurb.
  4. 4OrnamentOne drawn rule between the pitch and the footer: the Coven's braid, Snide's censor strip, the Everymen cog, Albescent's moving spectrum. Hidden from screen readers. No catalog key.
  5. 5StandingOne short line for where you stand: locked, eligible or member. Source: status.*.
  6. 6HeadcountAlways directly after the standing, never in the button. Shown only when the count is known. Source: members.
  7. 7CallThe button, full width, last. It holds its label and nothing else. Source: cta.

Fixed for everyone

  • Which slots exist, and their order
  • Which catalog key feeds each slot
  • Band, body, footer as the three zones; footer pinned to the bottom
  • The headcount sits beside the standing; the seal sits in the band

The faction's to dress

  • Ground, texture, border, corner radius, tilt
  • Display and text faces, sizes within the slot
  • Band as a row or a crest; left or centred text
  • The mark, the ornament, the seal's drawing
  • The button's shape and paint

The same fields also sit in different places

  • Member countIts own corner on Coven and Singularity. Joined to the status with a dot on UA, WOW, Ephemerists and the default. Inside the button on Snide and Everymen.
  • TaglineUnder the name in the body on most. Inside the header on Ephemerists and Snide, where Snide sets it at 10px. First line of the body on Everymen.
  • NameIn the header on five. In the body, under a header that holds only the mark, on UA. Under the mark with no header at all on WOW.
  • MembershipOnly WOW shows a visible mark when you belong. Everyone else changes one line of small text.
What moves

Per faction

UA

The name joins the enso on the mandala field. "Make it anyway" becomes the display line. The "UA" wordmark leaves the copy and comes back as the member seal, cut like an artist's chop. Needs a locked line, or see decision 1.

Cozy Coven

Closest to the contract already. The motto is lettered in the same hand as the name. Braid, status and count stay where they are.

Warriors of Whimsy

Gains a pitch and an ornament, a row of harlequin diamonds. The CHOSEN sash keeps its ribbon and loses its word, so it is a drawing like everyone else's seal.

S.N.I.D.E.

The tagline leaves the black bar, where it was 10px, and becomes a marker scrawl across the note. The count leaves the button and sits beside the status.

The Ephemerists

The tagline leaves the header and becomes the display line under the double rule. Gains an ornament: a tick scale, as on the plate's instruments.

Singularity

Already fits. The motto gets a cursor, and the ornament is a row of process lamps.

Everymen

The name is truly centred in the mast. The motto takes the big poster setting that "PICK UP THE LOAD" had. Gains a pitch. The count leaves the button and takes the plaque's small olive caps.

Albescent

Keeps the spectrum: the rainbow edge, the labyrinth turning in its colours, and the moving rule, which is now its ornament. Redaction blanks the copy and leaves every slot standing.

Yours to decide

Three calls before anything is built

  1. Does a locked tile say anything?You removed UA's locked line as not needed. Either every faction has one, or the standing slot is empty for everyone when locked. The mock shows everyone with one.
  2. What happens to the Everymen's two extra lines?I'd retire both and let the motto carry the shout, as mocked. The alternative is a "shout" slot every faction must fill, which is eight new lines of copy.
  3. Does every faction draw a member seal?I'd say yes: it is the one thing on the directory that tells you at a glance which tile is yours, and it costs no copy. Flip to Member to see all eight.

How this fits the repo's rule against unifying archetypes: each faction keeps its own card file and its own tree. What they share is a chassis of layout blocks (band, body, footer), the way the task cards share the masthead.
