Gold adjacency — Warriors of Whimsy
UA primary is a red-shifted burnt sienna (#C24A18, hue ~18°), not a yellow-orange. Against WoW gold (~45–50°) the two are >25° apart in hue and differ in luminance, so a 10 px faction dot, a rainbow-bar stripe, and a filter pennant all stay legibly distinct at small sizes. Rule: UA never uses a value lighter than --glow for the dot; gold is reserved entirely for WoW.

the sigil · enso

The ensō

A single hand-drawn brushstroke circle in orange. Inline SVG — there is no asset pipeline. The two details that get lost in translation to code, spelled out below: the weight variation along the stroke and the open gap.

the gap ↙ ↘ the press

One stroke, ~330° arc. Two overlapping <path> arcs on the same circle, both rotated -7° so the axis reads organic, not upright. stroke-linecap:round on both.

Weight variation is the signature. The heavy arc (the long sweep) is stroke-width 22 — the press, where the brush loads at ~1 o'clock. A second, lighter arc closes the tail at stroke-width 10, stroke-opacity .85 — the lift, where the brush runs dry. Never one uniform width; the taper from 22→10 is what makes it a brushstroke and not a ring.

The gap is left open at the lower-left (~7–8 o'clock) — the two arcs stop short of meeting. Colour is --faction-ua-glow (#DD5A1E). Reserved for the score and the faction mark — never used as a container border. Files: enso.svg (mark) · enso-detailed.svg (textured score ring).

the pattern · mandala

Mandala geometry — one primitive, three strengths

Concentric radial pattern is expensive to hand-build per surface, so it is one parameterized SVG primitive — Mandala({ rings, size, hue, opacity }) — the generalized form of the vote-cell renderer already in this file. Rank drives ring count; every surface calls the same primitive at one of three strengths.

Full strength
The vote control only
Foreground, animated, interactive. Each 1–5 rank is a mandala that grows more rings, warmer, and fuller as it rises. This is the one place the pattern earns full detail. See vote.
Faint texture
6–22% behind surfaces
Bled off a corner, low opacity, static: card grounds, the page backdrop, faction hero, the avatar ring. Present but never competing with text. (The lotus corner-bleed on the cards is this tier.)
Absent
Dense / text surfaces
Feed rows, comments, edit-form fields, filter tabs, the level pill. These stay clean — a dashed orange rule or a single ensō dot is the only ornament. Cheap to build, calm to read.

§10 · page backdrop · backdrop

Page backdrop

Mesa-sand ground (--faction-ua-page) with one very faint ensō/mandala bleed off a corner — it must sit behind unrelated content without fighting it. It themes a page only when UA is that page's single context; on any mixed or neutral page it degrades to the global rainbow watercolour (never theme a mixed page to one faction).
