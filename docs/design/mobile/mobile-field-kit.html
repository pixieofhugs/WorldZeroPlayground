World Zero · Mobile Field Kit

A phone-native language, tinted per faction from one layout.

375px, one-hand. Every screen is built from a single markup layer and painted by a token cascade: theme (light / dark) sets the neutral surfaces; treatment (faction slug) overrides accent, headline face, paper and voice. No brand colors are invented — everything below resolves to --faction-* / --color-* tokens already in the kit. Two treatments shipped here: Default (na) — Lora + the seven-faction rainbow — and Everymen — Special Elite, amber (--everymen-gold) on paper, field-report voice.

02

FieldDesk — Home

The logged-in dashboard. Character (avatar, name, level, faction, points), active tasks tappable into detail, and two primary actions — browse tasks, post praxis — kept in the bottom third for one-hand reach. Sits above the tab bar. Tap the tabs; press a task row. Default treatment, light + dark.

03

Character paths

Default treatment, no faction — the paths for managing who you play as, read left → right. Create character is the first-run screen (no account yet, so no tab bar — just a sticky Create). To reach character select once you're in the app, tap Switch on the Home character card (or your avatar/name — the ▾ caret); that opens the switcher sheet, which also holds Create new character and Edit. (Updates screen parked — those cards are faction-based and a separate effort.)

04

Tasks

Browse is a scannable card list with phone-native filter chips (horizontal scroll) + a Sort button that opens a bottom sheet — no desktop sidebar. Each card carries faction, points, difficulty and slots. Detail is a pushed screen with a sticky Sign-up bar, submitted proofs, and comments below.

05

Praxis

The proof loop. Feed of media-forward proof cards (author, faction, thumbnail, star rating) → detail with full media, a big touch-sized star vote, and comments. The composer is the most important flow: a focused full-screen editor with Write/Preview toggle, an obvious media-add grid, a formatting accessory bar riding the keyboard, and a submit affordance that stays reachable.

06

Players

Directory / leaderboard of players (rank, avatar, faction, level, points) → a public profile with stats and a Praxis/Tasks toggle over a thumbnail grid.

07

Factions

Browsed by an Unaffiliated player: a grid of the factions (each in its own color) with a “you're unaffiliated” banner, → a faction page whose hero wears that faction's color while the app chrome stays neutral, with a Join action. (Committing to a faction is what re-skins the whole app — the treatment work.)

08

Moments & utility

The connective tissue: a whimsical level-up celebration (rainbow burst + confetti), global search, and settings (characters, notifications, appearance, sign out).
